📖 Calculator User Guide

🔮 Potentials & Bonus Potentials

Configure equipment, tier, and lines, then define your target thresholds:

  • Static Option Rows: Every possible stat is listed. Type in the combined minimum value you wish to target (e.g., enter 18 for 18% Crit DMG).
  • Value Guide Table (Left Panel): Displays the maximum possible rolls for 1, 2, and 3 combined lines to help guide your inputs.
  • Impossible Combinations: The calculator automatically verifies if your combination is mathematically possible (e.g., asking for 30% Crit DMG on a 2-line item is flagged as impossible).

🔥 Rebirth Flames

For Rebirth Flames, configure the target lines and stats:

  • Dropdown Rows: Select your target stats from the dropdowns (up to 2 lines).
  • Min Values: Specify the minimum roll threshold for each selected stat.
  • Scales with X: Select generic scaling properties (e.g. PHY ATK scales with X) where X is the sum of all other stats combined. Specifying a min value is disabled for scaling stats.

💰 Cost Estimates

Get estimated costs to hit your combination at various percentiles:

  • Meso Costs: Input the cost of a single Red Cube in Millions (M) (e.g. type 12.5 for 12,500,000 mesos) under the "Cost per Cube" config.
  • Crystal Costs: Automatically calculated using 25 crystals per cube for Potentials and 50 crystals per cube for Bonus Potentials.
  • Results Grid: Costs are calculated and displayed directly inside the 50% (Median), 75th, 85th, and 95th percentile cards under the rolls required.

🎲 Calculating

Once your targets and costs are configured, click the 🎲 Calculate Odds button to generate the success rates, required rolls, and costs.
